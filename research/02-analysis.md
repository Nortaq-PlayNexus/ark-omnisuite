## Research hypotheses, not proven ecosystem-wide gaps
1. Unified rate configuration across independent tools.
2. User-owned, shareable map marker sets.
3. Transparent defense checklist scoring.
4. Task recommendations based on a player's constraints.
5. A single manually maintained server-event timeline.
6. Player-maintained lost tame board.
7. A consolidated ancestor and mutation-counter view.
8. Boss preparation lists customized by a tribe.
9. Offline-first planning.
10. Accessible keyboard operation.
11. A clear boundary between validated and unverified game data.
12. Common calculations shared between web and bot.
13. Configurable rates per cluster.
14. A provenance label for imported recipes.
15. A portable export of personal planning data.

These are opportunities for investigation, not claims that no competitor provides them or that these concepts are original. Previous assertions about competitors' missing features, failure causes, comparative rankings, and universal visual debt lacked sufficient evidence and are withdrawn.

## Important reference disagreement
Dododex displays 57:59 for a level-150 Rex with Exceptional Kibble at its displayed defaults (https://www.dododex.com/taming/rex); another calculator displays 17 and 35m25s (https://dodoreaper.com/ark-survival-evolved/tools/taming-calculator). Settings and rates must be reproduced before comparing results. The earlier invented formulas and seed data are not validated and are removed. The only fixed game-data calculation in this prototype is 2 Metal per Metal Ingot (https://ark.fandom.com/wiki/Metal_Ingot).

## Newly verified limitation on gap claims
ARK Savegame Visualiser advertises custom map markers, creature ancestry, tribe logs and Discord bot commands (https://survivetheark.com/index.php?%2Fforums%2Ftopic%2F491802-admin-trusted-player-tool-game-save-visualiser%2F=). These capabilities are not unprecedented.
