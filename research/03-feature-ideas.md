# Additional feature research — 23 November 2026

This is a focused search, not an exhaustive ecosystem survey. Competitors below demonstrate that the ideas are **not necessarily original**. Verify rates and game-version differences before applying in-game.

| Idea / player job | Evidence & existing solutions | Assessment / constraints |
|---|---|---|
| Compare inherited breeding stats across multiple attempts | Official wiki states offspring initially inherit the stronger stat with 55% probability, before mutation: https://ark.wiki.gg/wiki/Breeding and https://ark.wiki.gg/wiki/Mutations. Existing ARKTools advertises a 55/45 breeding calculator: https://github.com/freebiodata/arctools. | Useful, not novel. Model independent selection of specified *different* parental stats; identical values are not 55% events. Do not model mutations or imprint bonuses. Twins/triplets from one event are not independent attempts. |
| Plan refining forge fuel for a metal run | Forge burn times and 20 seconds / ingot are documented at https://ark.fandom.com/wiki/Refining_Forge and https://ark.fandom.com/wiki/Metal_Ingot. Arkbuddy already offers a forge calculator: https://arkbuddy.app/tools. | Useful, not novel. Display rounding, baseline and exclusions; fuel selection only one type, no extra fuel burn or concurrent gasoline. |
| Consolidate a multi-item crafting queue, subtract stock once | Arkbuddy already combines shared ingredients, supports custom recipes, and explicitly warns its ASE/ASA catalogue is not separately verified: https://arkbuddy.app/tools/crafting-calculator ; community requests for custom crafting costs: https://www.reddit.com/r/ARK/comments/1jgn1py/made_a_new_crafting_calculator_link_in_comments/ . | Useful, not novel. Reuse the existing five sourced ASE variants; aggregate duplicate recipe quantities before rounding Chemistry Bench batches. Avoid promising broad catalog coverage or consuming shared stock twice. |
| Breeding maturation and imprint schedule | Dodoreaper https://dodoreaper.com/ark-survival-evolved-ascended/tools/breeding-calculator and ASA-focused https://arktoolbox.net/ark-imprint-calculator ; rates explained at https://arktoolbox.net/ark-breeding-calculator. | Defer species estimates: no sufficiently verified/versioned ASE baseline dataset and rate preset; current numbers must not be guessed. |
| Personal breeding library and pedigree | ARK Smart Breeding already has a library and pedigree view: https://github.com/cadon/ARKStatsExtractor. | Existing ledger is text-only; structured family graphs and safe imports deferred until schema and privacy are designed. |
| Visual painting / creature color planning | Arkbuddy advertises creature color preview and PNT converter: https://arkbuddy.app/ . | Defer until licensed/versioned color data and formats can be validated. |
| Base-building and raid planner | Arkbuddy resource calculator and base-builder plans: https://arkbuddy.app/tools/crafting-calculator ; PVP strategy guide: https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=1563393561 . | Defer claims of accurate effectiveness; server rules and opposing loadouts vary, and existing readiness checklist is not a raid simulation. |
| Server-save discovery / locating tames | Savegame Visualiser describes save-derived maps and Discord commands: https://survivetheark.com/index.php?%2Fforums%2Ftopic%2F491802-admin-trusted-player-tool-game-save-visualiser%2F= . | Defer until authorized save ingestion, privacy and security are designed; do not advertise manual waypoint records as automatic discovery. |

**New candidates from this wave:** ARKTools (https://github.com/freebiodata/arctools), ArkDino (https://arkdino.com/), ARK Toolbox (https://arktoolbox.net/tools). They warrant independent assessment and may be primarily ASA for some modules. This wave produced new candidates; exhaustive-discovery termination remains unmet.
