# Broader survivor-workflow research

Date: 2026-11-23. Fifteen targeted web searches covered taming, breeding, forge/fuel, boss tribute, preservation, farming, waypoint/note tools, server administration, cryopods and save data. This is a focused expansion—not a literal 100× study, proof of global coverage, or proof of novelty. Search results are leads; primary links below document the claims selected for implementation.

## Mechanic and competitor findings

1. **Boss tributes.** The Island Broodmother Arena lists the Clever, Hunter and Massive artifacts plus four apex drops in difficulty-dependent quantities: https://ark.wiki.gg/wiki/Broodmother_Arena. Other maps and modded servers may differ. Source pages disagree on beta player-level requirements, so the prototype does not assert a level requirement: compare https://ark.wiki.gg/wiki/Broodmother_Lysrix with https://ark.fandom.com/wiki/Broodmother_Lysrix.
2. **Breeding/incubation.** Dododex has an egg incubation planner and schedules: https://www.dododex.com/breeding. The Egg Incubator adjusts temperature manually and eggs can have different temperature ranges: https://ark.wiki.gg/wiki/Egg_Incubator. A Raptor Egg's displayed temperature and duration are documented at https://ark.wiki.gg/wiki/Raptor_Egg. Species-specific data, versions and actual temperature mechanics should not be guessed. Powered incubator behavior/boost differs from simply holding a room at a temperature.
3. **Raising and imprint.** Imprint timing depends on creature-specific maturation, server settings and rounding: https://ark.fandom.com/wiki/Imprinting. Existing tools include https://wikily.gg/ark-survival-evolved/dinosaurs/doedicurus/breeding-calculator and https://arktoolbox.net/ark-imprint-calculator. Avoid assuming ASA data/formulas work unchanged in ASE.
4. **Starve and torpor.** Existing Dododex timers: https://www.dododex.com/taming/spinosaur; older taming mechanics explanation: https://crumplecorn.com/content/taming-calculator. Narcotics apply over time; a simple linear drain is not a full safety simulation. Do not advertise an unverified universal tame calculator.
5. **Route and notes.** The official wiki supplies locations and collection marking https://ark.wiki.gg/wiki/Explorer_Map/The_Island; existing in-game tracker mod https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=1631378184 and Arkbuddy note tracker https://arkbuddy.app/tools. Straight grid distance is not terrain, travel time, real-world distance or an in-game GPS conversion.
6. **Crafting.** The official recipe reference lists crop plots and stations: https://ark.wiki.gg/wiki/Crafting. Arkbuddy already has queues and custom blueprints https://arkbuddy.app/tools/crafting-calculator. Current prototype remains a very small recipe subset.
7. **Freshness.** Raw meat spoils one item at a time in a stack, with container-dependent timers: https://ark.fandom.com/wiki/Raw_Meat. Implementing a generic single stack-expiry countdown would be misleading.
8. **Server settings.** GameUserSettings.ini and Game.ini are distinct and have different sections https://zap-hosting.com/guides/docs/ark-configuration/. INI diff is a known capability: https://github.com/CyberJrod/ASA-Dedicated-Server-Tools. Back up and stop the server before applying changes.
9. **Save-file tooling.** ARK Smart Breeding has creature library, lineage, breeding and timer tools: https://github.com/cadon/ARKStatsExtractor. Savegame Visualiser exposes save-derived maps and requires authorized saves: https://survivetheark.com/index.php?%2Fforums%2Ftopic%2F491802-admin-trusted-player-tool-game-save-visualiser%2F=. Neither is evidence that OMNISUITE has equivalent live access.
10. **Forge caveat.** Sources differ on industrial forge fuel figures: https://ark.fandom.com/wiki/Industrial_Forge versus https://ark.wiki.gg/wiki/Industrial_Forge. Do not silently extend the existing ASE refining-forge planner to industrial assumptions.
11. **Cryopods.** Variations by mode and server configuration, plus contradictory guides, make automatic expiry or cryosickness claims hazardous: https://survivetheark.com/index.php?%2Fforums%2Ftopic%2F378654-cryopod-cryofridge-questions=.
12. **RCON.** Historical tools already cover schedules and logs: https://steamcommunity.com/app/346110/discussions/0/530646715638980889/?ctp=3. The prototype's local first-packet CLI is not a secured, complete RCON dashboard.

## Ideas / implementation decisions

- **Tribute Manifest:** calculate sourced The Island Broodmother requirements and gaps against user-entered stock, for Gamma/Beta/Alpha and repeated runs. Useful planning, not claimed novel.
- **Incubation Range Mixer:** compare user-entered closed temperature ranges, find a point in the largest overlap and show which ranges include it. No species dataset, hatch speed or egg-safety prediction; not a claim to exceed Dododex.
- **Waypoint Route Sketch:** find a shortest grid-distance ordering for user-entered same-map pins with a chosen starting coordinate. Explicitly ignore terrain, straight-line obstructions, movement speed, actual game distances and hazards. No live map or automated GPS.
- **Next candidates:** event overlap warnings, tribe logistics, versioned boss manifests per map, provenance-aware recipe imports, imprint planner with verified species/rate datasets, authenticated cluster services. Their novelty and feasibility remain open research questions.
